How every module works

Lecture, literature, then your own cases

Each module starts at the fundamentals and builds in difficulty. Lectures and case vignettes are paired with DICOM images and broken into short 5–7 minute clips that fit around a clinic schedule.

1

Didactic lecture

Expert-led video lectures and case vignettes reviewed alongside the actual DICOM images.

2

Research review

Review the module's assigned literature and submit a 350-word summary. About 5 hours per module.

3

Case submission

Analyze and submit 5 MRI studies related to the module's subject. About 5 hours per module.

  • Apply appropriate search patterns to ensure high-quality case assessment
  • Identify key anatomical landmarks, variations and abnormalities on imaging
  • Accurately interpret advanced imaging cases
  • Formulate definitive diagnoses and limited differentials

Course content is peer-reviewed for content validity by reviewers without conflicts of interest, to verify the material is evidence-based, fair, balanced and free from commercial bias.

Choose your path

One certification, or three courses at your own pace

Complete all 18 modules at once, or work toward certification one six-module course at a time. Finishing Courses I, II and III completes the Advanced Imaging Certification.

Questions

Advanced Imaging Certification: frequently asked questions

Who is the Advanced Imaging Certification for?

Doctors of chiropractic. It is an 18-module MRI curriculum built on case-based DICOM review, developed by the National Spine Management Group in collaboration with Medality™.

Can I take the imaging certification in parts?

Yes. You can complete all 18 modules in one enrollment, or work toward certification one six-module course at a time. Finishing Courses I, II and III completes the Advanced Imaging Certification.

How many CE hours is the certification?

276 CE hours across 18 modules: Course I is 87 hours, Course II is 92 hours and Course III is 97 hours.

Is it approved for continuing education?

Yes. The Advanced Imaging Certification is approved for continuing education through the Federation of Chiropractic Licensing Boards' PACE program, and National Spine Management Group is an FCLB PACE provider. Check with your state board for how PACE hours apply to your license renewal.

What does each module involve?

Each module has three parts: expert-led video lectures and case vignettes reviewed alongside the actual DICOM images; a research review of the module's assigned literature with a 350-word summary (about 5 hours); and 5 MRI case submissions related to the module's subject (about 5 hours). Lectures are broken into short 5–7 minute clips that fit around a clinic schedule.

How do I enroll?

Enrollment and purchases are completed through the NSMG learning portal, on the Advanced Imaging Certification page.
