Advanced Imaging Certification

The Spine Management Pathway: an 18-module MRI curriculum for doctors of chiropractic, built on case-based DICOM review and developed by the National Spine Management Group in collaboration with Medality™.

  • 18 modules
  • 3 six-module courses
  • 276 CE hours
  • 90 MRI cases submitted
About the Advanced Imaging Certification
Two-year post-doctoral fellowship for doctors of chiropractic

Fellowship in Spine Management

Imaging mastery in Year One, then a year of clinical rotations. Interprofessional rotations, coordinated within your own community with the help of NSMG, train chiropractors to work as spine-care navigators alongside radiology, orthopedics, neurosurgery and pain management.

  • 2 years, post-doctoral
  • 18 imaging modules in Year One
  • 6 clinical rotations in Year Two
  • 30+ peer-reviewed articles per rotation
